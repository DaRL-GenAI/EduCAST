Topic: Binary Numbers for Beginners

Learning goal: Help students understand why computers use binary and how to convert small decimals.

Outline:
1. Metaphor: light switches ON/OFF as 1/0.
2. Place values: 8-4-2-1.
3. Convert 13 to binary with a short Manim place-value animation.
4. Interactive check: multiple-choice "What is 1010 in decimal?"
5. Recap slide/motion graphic of key takeaways.

Audience: first-year CS undergrads, no prior digital logic.
